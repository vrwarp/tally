4. Check it before you walk away

  1. Open chrome://policy on the tablet, tap Reload policies, and confirm WebUsbAllowDevicesForUrls is listed with status OK. An error here means the value did not parse — that is the whole check on a tablet with no printer attached.
  2. On the one tablet that has the printer: revoke its old chooser grant first (Chrome → site settings → USB devices), or the next step passes for the wrong reason.
  3. Then open the kiosk with the printer plugged in. It should find it with no chooser and no set-up step.
