Who may sign in to Tally, and what they may do once they have. Changes apply to every phone immediately.

▸How Tally decides who may sign in

Tally decides access by Google address. An admin invites somebody here, they sign in with that Google account, and their profile appears beside this — from then on the profile decides what they may do. Some admins are pinned by the deployment itself and cannot be changed here; that is the way back in if access is ever lost.
